Score the set of 5 podcast scripts.

- angle_diversity: Are the 5 angles (technical, story, investment, beginner, contrarian) genuinely different in substance?
- conversation: Does it read like a real two-person conversation (reactions, interruptions), not an interview?
- hook: Does each script open with a line that makes a listener stay?
- accuracy: Are facts plausible and free of obvious errors?
- language_quality: Is the language natural for native speakers of the requested language (not translated-sounding), in the right script?
